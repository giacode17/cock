Blue Horizon PPO Gold — Summary of Benefits and Coverage (2026)

This is a summary of the Blue Horizon PPO Gold plan, underwritten by Blue Horizon. This plan carries a higher monthly premium in exchange for low, predictable copays and a low deductible.

Deductible: You must pay $500 (individual) or $1,000 (family) out of pocket each plan year before the plan shares costs for most non-copay services. Most routine services (PCP, urgent care, telehealth, specialist visits) use a flat copay instead and are not subject to the deductible.

Out-of-pocket maximum: Once you have paid $4,000 (individual) in a plan year, including your deductible, copays, and coinsurance, Blue Horizon PPO Gold pays 100% of covered in-network services for the rest of the year.

Primary care visits: $25 copay per visit, not subject to the deductible.

Urgent care visits: $60 copay per visit, not subject to the deductible. This is one of this plan's strongest advantages over a high-deductible plan — you know the cost up front regardless of when in the year the visit happens.

Emergency room visits: $350 copay per visit, then the plan covers the rest at 100% for in-network facilities. If you are later admitted to the hospital from the ER, the ER copay is waived and inpatient benefits apply instead. Emergency services are covered at in-network cost-sharing levels even at out-of-network facilities.

Telehealth visits: $10 copay per visit. Blue Horizon's telehealth partner is available 24/7 and is a low-cost first stop for non-emergency symptoms.

Coinsurance: For services beyond flat-copay categories (e.g. outpatient surgery, imaging), after the $500 deductible you pay 10% coinsurance; the plan pays 90%, up to your out-of-pocket maximum.

Network: This plan uses the Blue Horizon National PPO network, one of the broadest networks offered, and includes out-of-network coverage at a reduced benefit level (you pay more, but it is still partially covered, unlike an HMO).

What's covered: Preventive care (no cost), primary and specialist visits, urgent care, emergency care, telehealth, prescription drugs (tiered copay structure), outpatient and inpatient surgery, and mental health/substance use services at parity with medical/surgical benefits. No referral is required to see a specialist.

What's excluded: Cosmetic procedures not medically necessary, experimental treatments not approved by the plan's medical policy, and long-term custodial care.

Common medical events and what you pay: a routine PCP visit is a flat $25; an urgent care visit for a minor injury is a flat $60; an ER visit for a significant injury or illness is a flat $350 copay regardless of the total bill, as long as the facility is in-network.

Because most routine and urgent categories use flat copays rather than deductible-then-coinsurance, this plan produces much more predictable per-visit costs than a high-deductible plan, especially for people who use urgent care or the ER more than once or twice a year — the tradeoff is a higher monthly premium.
