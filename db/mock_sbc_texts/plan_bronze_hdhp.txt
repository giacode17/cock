Summit Bronze HDHP — Summary of Benefits and Coverage (2026)

This is a summary of the Summit Bronze High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP), underwritten by Summit Health Alliance. This plan pairs a lower monthly premium with a higher deductible, and is designed to be paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA).

Deductible: You must pay $3,500 (individual) or $7,000 (family) out of pocket each plan year before the plan begins to share costs for most services, except preventive care, which is covered at no cost regardless of whether you've met your deductible.

Out-of-pocket maximum: Once you have paid $7,000 (individual) in a plan year, including your deductible, copays, and coinsurance, Summit Bronze HDHP pays 100% of covered in-network services for the rest of the year.

Primary care visits: Subject to the deductible. Once your deductible is met, you pay 20% coinsurance for primary care physician (PCP) visits; the plan pays the remaining 80%.

Urgent care visits: Subject to the deductible. Once your deductible is met, you pay 20% coinsurance for urgent care visits. Because the full cost of an urgent care visit is applied to your deductible before coinsurance kicks in, early-year urgent care visits can be expensive out of pocket.

Emergency room visits: Subject to the deductible, then 20% coinsurance. Emergency services are covered whether the facility is in-network or out-of-network, but you are strongly encouraged to use in-network emergency rooms whenever it is safe to do so, since out-of-network facilities may bill you separately for any difference between their charge and the plan's allowed amount.

Telehealth visits: Subject to the deductible, then 20% coinsurance. Many telehealth vendors charge a flat visit fee, typically $40-$75, before insurance discount.

Coinsurance: After you meet your deductible, you and Summit Bronze HDHP share the cost of covered services. You pay 20% of the allowed amount; the plan pays 80%, up to your out-of-pocket maximum.

Network: This plan uses the Summit National PPO network. Using out-of-network providers may result in substantially higher costs, and some out-of-network services may not be covered at all except in emergencies.

What's covered: Preventive care (no cost, even before deductible), primary and specialist visits, urgent care, emergency care, telehealth, prescription drugs (subject to a separate drug deductible), outpatient surgery, and mental health/substance use services at parity with medical/surgical benefits.

What's excluded: Cosmetic procedures not medically necessary, experimental treatments not approved by the plan's medical policy, and long-term custodial care.

Common medical events and what you pay after the deductible: a routine PCP visit is typically $30-$60 in coinsurance; an urgent care visit for something like a minor injury is typically $150-$300 in coinsurance before the deductible is met, or 20% after; an ER visit for a significant injury or illness commonly runs $1,000-$3,000 in total allowed charges, of which you owe the full amount until your deductible is met, then 20% coinsurance up to your out-of-pocket maximum.

Because this plan has a high deductible, your costs earlier in the plan year — before you've met the $3,500 deductible — will generally be higher than a plan with lower deductibles and flat copays, especially for ER and urgent care visits. This plan tends to work best for people who are generally healthy and use few services, or who want to pair it with an HSA for tax-advantaged savings.
