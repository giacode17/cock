Cascade Silver HMO — Summary of Benefits and Coverage (2026)

This is a summary of the Cascade Silver HMO plan, underwritten by Cascade Care Network. This plan has a moderate premium and moderate deductible, and requires you to choose a primary care physician (PCP) who coordinates and refers your care within the Cascade network.

Deductible: You must pay $1,500 (individual) or $3,000 (family) out of pocket each plan year before the plan shares costs for most non-copay services. As with most HMOs, many routine services use a flat copay instead and are not subject to the deductible.

Out-of-pocket maximum: Once you have paid $5,500 (individual) in a plan year, including your deductible, copays, and coinsurance, Cascade Silver HMO pays 100% of covered in-network services for the rest of the year.

Primary care visits: $30 copay per visit, not subject to the deductible. You must select a PCP within the Cascade network; specialist visits generally require a referral from your PCP.

Urgent care visits: $75 copay per visit, not subject to the deductible.

Emergency room visits: $400 copay per visit, then 15% coinsurance after the deductible is met for any additional charges. Emergency care is covered even outside the Cascade network, since HMO out-of-network rules do not apply to true emergencies, but non-emergency care received out-of-network is generally not covered at all under this plan.

Telehealth visits: $15 copay per visit, available through the Cascade Care virtual visit line.

Coinsurance: For services beyond flat-copay categories (e.g. outpatient surgery, advanced imaging), after the $1,500 deductible you pay 15% coinsurance; the plan pays 85%, up to your out-of-pocket maximum.

Network: This is an HMO (Health Maintenance Organization) plan using the Cascade regional network only. Except for emergencies, care received from providers outside the Cascade network is not covered — you must use in-network providers and, for most specialist care, get a referral from your assigned PCP first.

What's covered: Preventive care (no cost), primary care visits, referred specialist visits, urgent care, emergency care, telehealth, prescription drugs (tiered copay structure), and mental health/substance use services at parity with medical/surgical benefits.

What's excluded: Non-emergency out-of-network care, cosmetic procedures not medically necessary, experimental treatments not approved by the plan's medical policy, and long-term custodial care. Specialist visits without a required PCP referral may be denied or billed at a higher out-of-pocket rate.

Common medical events and what you pay: a routine PCP visit is a flat $30; an urgent care visit for a minor injury is a flat $75; an ER visit for a significant injury or illness is a flat $400 copay plus 15% coinsurance on any amount above the copay after the deductible is met.

Because this plan requires in-network care and PCP referrals, it generally costs less than a broad PPO for people who are comfortable coordinating care through a single primary doctor and staying within the Cascade network, but offers less flexibility if you travel often or want to see specialists without a referral.
